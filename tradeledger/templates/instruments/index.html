What is a contract multiplier?

It converts a price move into money:

P&L = price move × position size × contract multiplier

If you record EUR/USD position size in standard lots and your broker's lot is 100,000 euros, a move from 1.08500 to 1.08900 (0.004) on 1 lot is 0.004 × 1 × 100,000 = 400 USD.

If you record size in single units instead, the multiplier is 1 and the size is 100,000. Either works, as long as size and multiplier use the same definition.

Instruments cannot be edited yet: changing a multiplier would silently change the result of every past trade on it.
